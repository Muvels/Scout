# Official Scout Builds

This document applies only to binary artifacts that Scout's maintainers publish
and identify, through a project-controlled signature or release channel, as an
"Official Scout Build." It does not change the source licenses in `LICENSE` or
any third-party component's license.

## Permission to use

The copyright holders grant recipients permission to install and run Official
Scout Builds, without charge, for lawful personal use and internal business
operations.

## Official distribution

Only Scout-maintainer channels and mirrors approved in writing may distribute
artifacts represented as Official Scout Builds. An approved mirror must provide
the exact, unmodified, signed artifact together with all accompanying license
and attribution material.

The source licenses still permit recipients to create and distribute their own
noncommercial builds when they satisfy the applicable terms. Such builds must
not be represented as official, must not reuse an official signature, and must
follow `TRADEMARKS.md`.

This distribution rule applies only to Scout-authored material, official Scout
signatures, and Scout project marks. It does not restrict extraction, use,
modification, or redistribution of TBF, Chromium, ungoogled-chromium, uBlock
Origin, or any other third-party component where that component's own license
grants those rights.

## No warranty

As far as the law allows, Official Scout Builds are provided as is, without any
warranty or condition. The copyright holders are not liable for damages arising
from their use or nature under any kind of legal claim.
