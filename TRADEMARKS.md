# Scout Project Marks

Scout's source licenses grant copyright and patent permissions only. They do not
grant permission to use Scout's names, logos, icons, signatures, or distinctive
visual identity as branding for another product, except for the descriptive
uses below and any rights that applicable law provides independently.

## Descriptive use

You may make truthful, non-confusing statements such as:

- "based on Scout";
- "a fork of Scout"; or
- "compatible with Scout."

Descriptive references must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or
official status. They should identify the original Scout project and, when
practical, link to its official project page.

## Uses requiring permission

Without prior written permission, do not:

- use "Scout" or a confusingly similar term as the name of a fork, product,
  service, company, domain, or package;
- use Scout's logo, application icon, signing identity, or distinctive trade
  dress for a fork or third-party distribution;
- describe a modified or independently built artifact as an Official Scout
  Build; or
- suggest that Scout's maintainers support or approve a fork, product, or
  service.

These rules concern Scout project marks only. Chromium, uBlock Origin, TBF, and
other third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners and are
governed separately.
